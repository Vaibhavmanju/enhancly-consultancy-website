Built to match people with the right opportunity — not just the next one.

Enhancly Consultancy is an HR and research consultancy working across B2B workforce solutions, B2C career placement and healthcare staffing, with dedicated placement corridors into the UK,Canada and Germany.

Our story

Started with a simple frustration: hiring shouldn't feel like guesswork.

Too many placements fail in the first ninety days because the brief was rushed or the candidate never really understood the role. Enhancly was built to slow that first step down — understand the team, the workload and the person — so the match holds.

Today that same discipline runs through three practices: workforce solutions for businesses, career placement for individuals, and healthcare staffing for practices and clinics — backed by a research team that keeps our sourcing and screening grounded in real data.

What we stand for

Mission, vision & the values behind the process.

Vision

Our approach

Three commitments on every assignment.

01

We scope before we source

A short discovery call with every client or candidate before any outreach begins — so the shortlist is relevant from message one.

02

We screen for the role, not the résumé

Structured screening against the day-to-day of the job, including compliance checks for regulated healthcare roles.

03

We stay through onboarding

Check-ins through the first few months, because a placement isn't finished the day someone signs an offer letter.
